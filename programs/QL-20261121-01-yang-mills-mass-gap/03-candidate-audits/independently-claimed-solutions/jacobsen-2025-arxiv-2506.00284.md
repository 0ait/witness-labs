## Main claim (verbatim abstract excerpt)
> "we present a constructive proof that pure SU(3) Yang-Mills theory on R^4 exists as a nontrivial Wightman quantum field theory and exhibits a strictly positive mass gap. … A convergent joint polymer expansion on the Wilson lattice provides uniform control of the continuum (a->0) and infinite-volume (L->inf) limits …"

## First-pass defects
1. **Group scope.** The claim covers SU(3) only. Jaffe–Witten requires every compact simple G (PO-14 unaddressed).
2. **Implausible central step.** It claims a *convergent* polymer expansion that controls a → 0 uniformly. Polymer expansions converge at strong coupling. The continuum limit of an asymptotically free theory needs g → 0 multiscale RG (Balaban-type). Single-expansion "uniform control" is the open problem itself (PO-11/12).
3. **Size.** The full source is 23 KB, which cannot plausibly contain the required estimates.
4. **Withdrawn** under arXiv's content-quality standard (a moderation decision, not peer review).

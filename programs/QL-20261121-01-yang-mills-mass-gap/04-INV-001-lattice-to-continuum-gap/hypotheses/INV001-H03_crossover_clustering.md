# INV001-H03: β-uniform clustering of block-spin effective measures at a fixed physical scale (hypothesis X of D08.3)
- **Label:** Hypothesis (Open problem in 4D).
- **Statement.** ME-R1 of the report: ∃ a_*, m_* > 0 such that the block-spin pushforward μ'_{β,L} at scale a_* clusters at rate m_* (coarse units), uniformly in β ≥ β₀ and L ≥ L₀(β), for a coarse-local family 𝒮'.
- **Role.** By D08.3, H03 + cyclicity ⇒ ME1. This is the R1 target.
- **Evidence.** 2D: holds after finitely many exact decimations (E01, D07); no 4D evidence. Obstacle: the crossover window between Balaban's small-coupling regime (S-119, couplings ≤ γ) and strong-coupling thresholds (S-106).
- **Depends on:** D08.

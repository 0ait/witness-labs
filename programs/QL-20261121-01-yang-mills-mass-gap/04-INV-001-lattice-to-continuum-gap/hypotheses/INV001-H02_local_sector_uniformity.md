# INV001-H02: local-sector gap uniform in volume at fixed weak coupling
- **Label:** Hypothesis.
- **Statement.** For each β ≥ β₀ there is m(β) > 0 with m^{loc}(β,L) ≥ m(β) for all L ≥ L₀(β), whereas m^{full}(β,L) → 0 or is dominated by torelon/flux states ∝ σ_lat·L at small L.
- **Role.** The WS04 half of ME1 (fixed β, L→∞). Via D04.2 it implies a unique vacuum in every subsequential infinite-volume limit.
- **Evidence.** Proved for |β| < 1/(16(d−1)) (SZZ Cor 1.6, lattice units, strong coupling only). Toy illustration: E02-B (Z₂ ladder, local gap bounded below in n, torelon gap ∝ n). No result at weak coupling for continuous G in 4D.
- **Depends on:** D02, D04.

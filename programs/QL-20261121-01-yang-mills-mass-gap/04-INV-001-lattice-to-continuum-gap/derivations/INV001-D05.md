# INV001-D05 — A positive lattice gap at every spacing can give a zero continuum gap (free field)

- **Status:** exact computation (Established, standard Gaussian lattice field theory). QUASAR's derivation is pending LATTICE verification. **Depends on:** D01, D02, D03.

## Model
Euclidean Gaussian lattice field on aℤ^{D}, D=d+1≥3, in infinite volume. In lattice units, the action is S=½Σ_x[Σ_μ(φ(x+μ̂)−φ(x))²+(m₀a)²φ(x)²], with m₀ the bare mass in physical units.

## Transfer-matrix spectrum (exact)
At spatial lattice momentum p, put M(p)² := (m₀a)² + Σ_{i=1}^d 4sin²(ap_i/2). The time-direction two-point function at fixed p is
  G_p(n) = ∫_{−π}^{π}(dk/2π) e^{ikn}/(2−2cos k+M²).
With z=e^{ik}: 2−z−z^{−1}+M² = −z^{−1}(z−e^{−E})(z−e^{E}), where cosh E = 1+M²/2 and E>0. The residue at z=e^{−E} (inside the unit circle) gives G_p(n) = e^{−E|n|}/(2 sinh E). Hence the transfer matrix (Fock space over one-particle states) has one-particle energies E(p) with cosh E(p)=1+M(p)²/2, i.e. sinh(E(p)/2)=M(p)/2. Multiparticle energies are sums. Its gap above the vacuum is
  m_lat(a) = inf_p E(p) = E(0) = 2 asinh(m₀a/2) > 0  for every a>0 when m₀>0.
So the physical gap is Δ(a) = m_lat/a = (2/a)asinh(m₀a/2), which satisfies m₀(1−(m₀a)²/24) ≤ Δ(a) ≤ m₀ (because x−x³/6 ≤ asinh x ≤ x for x≥0).

## Two regimes
1. **m₀ fixed:** m_lat = m₀a(1+O(a²)), exactly the scaling (4) of D03, and Δ(a)→m₀. The limit is the free field of mass m₀, and its Hamiltonian has σ(H)∩(0,m₀)=∅.
2. **m₀=m₀(a)→0 (at any rate, however slow):** m_lat(a)>0 for every a, yet Δ(a) ≤ m₀(a) → 0. The Schwinger functions of time-smeared fields converge to those of the massless free field (D≥3, where 1/k² is locally integrable, so the Fourier representations converge by dominated convergence). The massless free field's Hamiltonian has one-particle spectrum |p|, p∈ℝ^d, so σ(H)=[0,∞) and there is no gap.

**Conclusion.** "m_lat(β)>0 for every β" carries no continuum information. Only a lower bound proportional to a(β) with a fixed constant (D03 (4)) transfers. Moreover the same model shows the constant must be tied to the scale that makes the theory converge (here m₀ is that scale).

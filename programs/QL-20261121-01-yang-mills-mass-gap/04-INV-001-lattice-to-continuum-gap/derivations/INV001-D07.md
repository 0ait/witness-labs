# INV001-D07 — 2D Wilson lattice gauge theory: exact transfer-matrix spectrum and continuum trajectory

- **Status:** exact derivation; the closed forms are classical (Migdal-type character gluing). QUASAR's derivation is computationally cross-checked in E01 (Computationally verified finite result) and pending LATTICE verification. **Depends on:** character orthogonality on compact groups (Peter–Weyl).

1. **Character expansion.** For a class function w on G, w=Σ_r ĉ_rχ_r with ĉ_r=∫wχ̄_r dU. Write a_r := ĉ_r/d_r. For SU(2) and w=exp((β/2)Tr U): a_j = 2I_{2j+1}(β)/β (E01 RUN.md gives the integral). For U(1) and w=e^{β cos θ}: a_n=I_n(β).
2. **Gluing.** ∫χ_r(XU)χ_s(U^{−1}Y)dU = δ_{rs}χ_r(XY)/d_r (Schur orthogonality). On a closed oriented 2D lattice surface with V vertices, E links and n plaquettes, integrate links one at a time. Each integration identifies representations on adjacent plaquettes and contributes 1/d_r. After all links are integrated, the remaining vertex contractions contribute χ_r(1)=d_r each. So Z=Σ_r d_r^{n}a_r^{n}d_r^{−E}d_r^{V}=Σ_r d_r^{χ_E}a_r^n, with χ_E=V−E+n. Torus: χ_E=0, so Z=Σ_r a_r^n. This was checked for the 1×1 torus against direct 3D quadrature to ≤1.6e-16 (E01).
3. **Transfer matrix.** On the L-link spatial circle in temporal gauge, the gauge-invariant states are class functions of the spatial holonomy P, and one time step multiplies χ_r(P) by a_r^L. This follows from applying step 2 to a 1×L strip: L plaquettes glued along L−1 temporal links leave the product of the two boundary holonomies. Hence λ_r=a_r^L, consistent with Z=Tr T^{N_t}=Σ_r a_r^{LN_t}.
4. **Gaps.** m^{full}(β,L)=L·σ_lat(β), with σ_lat=−log(a_f/a_0) for the fundamental f. The eigenvectors χ_r(P), r≠0, are non-contractible (winding) states, so the local sector on the circle is ℂΩ_L: m^{loc}=+∞ (no local excitations). On the plane, plaquettes are i.i.d. under the Wilson measure (axial-gauge tree argument), so connected correlations of disjoint local observables vanish.
5. **Continuum.** The renormalisation condition σ_lat(β)=σ_phys·a² defines a(β)=(σ_lat(β)/σ_phys)^{1/2}~(3/(2βσ_phys))^{1/2} (SU(2)). On a circle of physical length ℓ: m^{full}=σ_phys ℓ a → 0, and Δ^{full}=σ_physℓ, finite but growing with ℓ (E01 table). The 2D theory is the textbook case where a lattice-unit gap → 0 ∝ a is compatible with a finite physical "gap", but the gap is a non-local flux energy. **This is not evidence about 4D.**

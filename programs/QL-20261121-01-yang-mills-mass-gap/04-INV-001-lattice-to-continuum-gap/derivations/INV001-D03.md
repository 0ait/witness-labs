# INV001-D03 — Scale obstruction: the lattice gap must vanish exactly like a(β)

- **Status:** the proofs are QUASAR's and elementary given D01/D02 (pending LATTICE verification). The perturbative formula for a(β) is labelled **Heuristic**.
- **Depends on:** D01, D02. **Used by:** report §4 (missing estimates).

## D03.1 (what lattice gaps must supply)
Along a sequence (β_k,L_k,a_k) with a_k→0, the lattice gap gives, through D02(a), the physical clustering rate m^{loc}(β_k,L_k)/a_k and nothing more. Hence:
- (i) **Sufficient for (U):** ∃Δ>0 ∀k: m^{loc}(β_k,L_k) ≥ Δ a_k.
- (ii) A bound uniform in lattice units, m^{loc}(β,L) ≥ m_* > 0 for all β in some set, gives m^{loc}/a ≥ m_*/a → ∞ *if* that set of β contains a sequence with a(β)→0. If the set is bounded (as for strong-coupling results, β ≤ β_max), it says nothing about a→0, because a(β) is bounded below on such a set. For 4D asymptotically free theories a(β)→0 requires β→∞ (see D03.3).

## D03.2 (a gap uniform in lattice units forces a trivial limit)
**Lemma.** Assume (C1) of D02(b), and that the limit S(F,F;t) is continuous in t∈[0,∞) for F∈𝒟 (hypothesis (T) of D01). If m^{loc}(β_k,L_k)/a_k → ∞, then for every F∈𝒟, S(F,F;t)=|s(F)|² for all t≥0. Consequently, in the OS reconstruction from 𝒟, QF̂=0 for all F∈𝒟, so ℋ=ℂΩ (the limit is trivial: every observable in 𝒟 is a c-number).

*Proof.* Fix t>0. By (3), |⟨F_k;F_k⟩_{n_k}| ≤ ‖QF_kΩ_k‖²e^{−(m_k/a_k)·a_kn_k}. Here ‖QF_kΩ_k‖² → c(F)² < ∞, a_kn_k→t>0 and m_k/a_k→∞, so the right side tends to 0. Hence S(F,F;t)−|s(F)|²=0 for every t>0, and by continuity also at t=0, i.e. c(F)²=‖QF̂‖²=0. ∎

**Corollary (two-sided window).** If the limit is non-trivial (some F∈𝒟 with QF̂≠0), then liminf_k m^{loc}(β_k,L_k)/a_k < ∞. If in addition (U) holds, then along a subsequence
  Δ·a_k ≤ m^{loc}(β_k,L_k) ≤ C·a_k  (0<Δ≤C<∞).  (4)
So the lattice-unit gap must go to zero at exactly the rate of the lattice spacing. Upper bound ⇔ finite correlation length, which is necessary for non-triviality. Lower bound ⇔ gap. The whole bridge difficulty is the *lower* half of (4), tied to the *same* scale a(β) that makes the other observables converge.

**Example (illustrates D03.2).** Adhikari–Cao 2025 (finite G, 4D) prove correlation decay at rate (β/2)Δ_G in lattice units at large β. This lattice-unit rate grows with β: finite-group lattice gauge theories freeze at weak coupling and have no non-trivial continuum limit, consistent with D03.2. Similarly, E02's single-plaquette gap tends to 4 in lattice units as g→0.

## D03.3 (what fixes a(β); the circularity trap)
a(β) is not an output of the lattice theory; it is fixed by a renormalisation condition, i.e. by declaring the physical value of one lattice-unit quantity μ_lat(β)→0:
  a(β) := μ_lat(β)/μ_phys.
Candidates for μ_lat: √σ_lat (string tension), 1/r₀ (Sommer scale), a gradient-flow scale 1/√t₀, or a running coupling held fixed at a box size (Lüscher–Weisz–Wolff type schemes). The last needs no infrared assumption.
- If μ_lat := m^{loc} itself, then (U) holds by definition. The content then moves entirely into (C1): non-triviality and convergence of all *other* correlations at that scale. No proof is gained.
- Therefore the invariant, scheme-independent content of "lattice gap ⇒ continuum gap" is: **for a lattice-unit scale μ_lat that defines a non-trivial convergent continuum limit, the dimensionless ratio m^{loc}(β,L)/μ_lat(β) has positive liminf as β→∞ and L·μ_lat(β)→∞.**
- *Heuristic (2-loop perturbation theory, not a theorem):* for SU(N), a(β)Λ_L ≈ (b₀g₀²)^{−b₁/(2b₀²)}exp(−1/(2b₀g₀²)), with g₀²=2N/β, b₀=11N/(3(4π)²) and b₁=34N²/(3(4π)⁴). If this holds, any non-trivial limit needs β→∞ with m^{loc} ≈ exp(−β/(4Nb₀)), up to powers. This is far outside every regime where a lattice gap is currently proved (all at β ≤ β_max; see the literature file).

# INV001-D08: decay along an arithmetic progression suffices, and the conditional block-spin crossover lemma (route R1, first derivation)

- **Status.** D08.1 and D08.2 are elementary proofs by QUASAR (proof pending LATTICE verification). D08.3 is a *conditional* proposition: its hypothesis (X) is an **Open problem** for 4D Yang–Mills and it does not hold by any cited result. Label for D08.3 as a whole: proposed conditional reduction (needs verification); it is NOT a gap result.
- **Depends on:** D01, D02. **Used by:** report route R1; ME-R1.

## D08.1 Lemma (progression decay ⇒ spectral gap on the generated subspace)
Let A be a positive self-adjoint contraction on a Hilbert space 𝒦. Let b∈ℕ, s∈ℕ₀ with s<b, μ>0, and let 𝒮⊂𝒦 be a set. Suppose that for each ψ∈𝒮 there is C_ψ<∞ with
  0 ≤ ⟨ψ, A^{nb−s}ψ⟩ ≤ C_ψ e^{−μn}  for all n≥1.
Let 𝒦₀ be the closed A-invariant subspace generated by 𝒮. Then ‖A|_{𝒦₀}‖ ≤ r := e^{−μ/b}, so ⟨φ,A^mφ⟩ ≤ ‖φ‖²r^m for all φ∈𝒦₀ and all m.

*Proof.* Let ν_ψ be the spectral measure of A at ψ, supported in [0,1]. Suppose ν_ψ((r',1]) = c > 0 for some r' > r. Then ⟨ψ,A^{nb−s}ψ⟩ = ∫λ^{nb−s}dν_ψ ≥ c r'^{nb−s}. But c r'^{nb−s}/(C_ψ e^{−μn}) = (c/C_ψ) r'^{−s}(r'/r)^{nb} → ∞, a contradiction. Hence ν_ψ((r,1]) = 0, i.e. E ψ = 0 with E := E_A((r,1]). E commutes with A and is linear and continuous, so it vanishes on span{A^jψ: ψ∈𝒮, j≥0} and on its closure 𝒦₀. On 𝒦₀ = E_A([0,r])𝒦₀ the spectral theorem gives ‖A|_{𝒦₀}‖ ≤ r. ∎

*Remarks.*
- The constant C_ψ disappears: the conclusion has constant ‖φ‖². This is the reason a gap needs a uniform *rate* but no uniform prefactor (compare D01).
- Decay along a sparse progression with rate μ per b steps yields rate μ/b per step. No regularity between the progression points is needed, because A ≥ 0. Positivity of the transfer matrix (Lüscher/OS78) is essential here.

## D08.2 Lemma (exact block reduction)
Let μ be a probability measure on a fine configuration space Ω, B:Ω→Ω' measurable, and μ' := B_*μ. Then for bounded measurable F',G' on Ω', E_μ[(F'∘B)(G'∘B)] = E_{μ'}[F'G']. Suppose B intertwines fine time translation by b^k steps with coarse translation by one step (τ'∘B = B∘τ^{b^k}) and the reflections (θ'∘B = B∘θ). Then coarse connected correlations of (θ'F', τ'_n F') equal fine connected correlations of (θ(F'∘B), τ_{nb^k}(F'∘B)). *Proof.* This is the change-of-variables formula. ∎

## D08.3 Proposition (conditional crossover lemma)
Fix b≥2 and a physical scale a_*>0. For β≥β₀ let k(β) be the integer with b^{k}a(β) ∈ [a_*, b a_*). Let B_β be a k-step block-spin map as in D08.2, built from gauge-covariant block averages so that gauge-invariant functions of coarse loops are gauge-invariant functions of fine links supported in a fine slab of time-thickness s_k < b^k. Let μ'_{β,L} be the pushforward of the Wilson measure. Assume

**(X) uniform coarse clustering.** There exist m_*>0 and a family 𝒮' of coarse-local gauge-invariant observables such that for all β≥β₀, L≥L₀(β), F'∈𝒮' and n≥1:
  0 ≤ ⟨θ'F'; τ'_nF'⟩_{μ'_{β,L}} ≤ C(F') e^{−m_*n}.

Then, writing ψ_{F'} for the OS/transfer-matrix vector of F'∘B_β and 𝒦_β for the closed T-invariant subspace generated by {Qψ_{F'}: F'∈𝒮'}, we have for all β≥β₀, L≥L₀(β):
  m(T|_{𝒦_β}) ≥ m_*/b^{k(β)} ≥ (m_*/(b a_*))·a(β),
i.e. (U) holds on 𝒦_β with Δ = m_*/(b a_*).

*Proof.* By D08.2, the fine correlation ⟨θ(F'∘B);τ_{nb^k}(F'∘B)⟩ equals the coarse one. By the transfer-matrix representation (D02(a), slab version) it equals ⟨Qψ_{F'},(T/λ₀)^{nb^k−s_k}Qψ_{F'}⟩, which is ≥0 by reflection positivity. Apply D08.1 with A = (T/λ₀)|_{Q𝒦}, b ↦ b^k, μ = m_*: ‖A|_{𝒦_β}‖ ≤ e^{−m_*/b^k}. Finally b^k ≤ b a_*/a(β). ∎

## What D08.3 reduces the problem to (honest account)
- **(X) is the whole difficulty.** It asks for a strong-coupling-type clustering theorem, uniform in β, for the *effective* measure at the fixed physical scale a_*. Its action is not Wilson's: it is non-local, with multi-plaquette and large-field terms. Known tools:
  - Strong-coupling theorems (Seiler S-105; SZZ S-106; CNS S-108/109) apply to Wilson-type actions with |β| below an explicit threshold (SZZ: |β| < 1/(16(d−1)) in their normalisation). A class of non-Wilson actions uniform in β is not covered by any cited statement.
  - Balaban (S-112…S-119) controls the effective actions only while the effective couplings stay in ]0,γ] with γ small, on a finite torus (S-119 Thm 1 hypothesis, verified quote).
  - The *crossover window* is the range of scales where g_k² is between γ and the strong-coupling threshold. There neither tool applies. **This window is the precise gap for R1** (ME-R1 in the report).
- **Cyclicity (O1).** 𝒦_β only contains states generated by observables of resolution ≥ a_*. Concluding a gap for the whole physical Hilbert space needs that observables smeared at scale ≥ a_* are cyclic in the continuum theory (Reeh–Schlieder-type; Hypothesis, since there is no theory yet). Alternatively, a_* must be sent to 0 with m_*/a_* bounded below, which is again a uniform estimate.
- **2D sanity check (Established, D07/E01).** In 2D, decimation is exact and the coarse measure is again a product of single-plaquette class functions with t ↦ t^{b²} (E01: from β=64, t_{1/2} = 0.97666 → 3.13e−11 after b=32). So (X) holds with any a_* after finitely many steps. This is consistent with the exact 2D local gap being infinite (D07). It gives no evidence about 4D, where there is no exact decimation.

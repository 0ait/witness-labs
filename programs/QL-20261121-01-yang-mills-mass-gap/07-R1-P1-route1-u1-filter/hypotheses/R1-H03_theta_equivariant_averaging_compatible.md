# R1-H03: Balaban's single-step estimates hold for a θ-equivariant averaging
- **Label:** Hypothesis (plausible; unverified).
- **Statement.** S-113-type averaging bounds and the S-112/115/116 propagator and variational estimates remain valid when the corner/fixed-contour average is replaced by the centre-based, reflection-symmetric contour multiset with a symmetric gauge-covariant rule (R1-D02 Prop. 5).
- **Evidence.**
  - BIJ [P]: "almost any definition of average would do" (large-field branch) and "the choice of gauge fix is not very important at this stage".
  - R1-E03: the replacement intertwines θ to round-off; the corner rule does not (non-abelian).
- **Filter status:** group-blind.
- **Depends on:** R1-D02, R1-E03.

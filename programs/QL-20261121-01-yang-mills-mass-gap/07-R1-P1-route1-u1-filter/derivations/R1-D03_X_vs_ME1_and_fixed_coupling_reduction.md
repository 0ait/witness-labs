# R1-D03: X is ME1 restricted to coarse observables; what R1 actually adds is a β-free reformulation

QL-20261121-01 · QUASAR · Route 1 phase 1 · 2026-11-21. Prop. 1 is an elementary proof by QUASAR (pending LATTICE). Prop. 2 is a conditional reduction (proof QUASAR's; its hypotheses are open).
- **Depends on:** D08.1–D08.3, D03.2, D03.3, R1-D01, R1-E02.

## Proposition 1 ("X is ME1 relabelled" holds at the level of statements; Proved here)
Fix B_β as in D08.3/R1-D02, with b^{k(β)} a(β) ∈ [a_*, b a_*). Let 𝒟_β := {F′∘B_β : F′ ∈ 𝒮′} and let 𝒦_β be the T-invariant subspace generated by Q𝒟_βΩ. Then the following are equivalent, up to constants:
- (X) ∃m_*>0 ∀β≥β₀, L≥L₀(β), F′∈𝒮′, n≥1: 0 ≤ ⟨θF̄′;τ′_nF′⟩_{μ′} ≤ C(F′,β) e^{−m_* n};
- (U|𝒦) ∃Δ>0 ∀β≥β₀, L≥L₀(β): m(T|𝒦_β) ≥ Δ·a(β).

*Proof.*
- (X)⇒(U|𝒦) is D08.3, with Δ = m_*/(b a_*).
- (U|𝒦)⇒(X): By D08.2 the coarse correlation at coarse distance n equals ⟨Qψ, (T/λ₀)^{nb^k − s}Qψ⟩. This is ≥ 0 because T ≥ 0, and it is ≤ ‖Qψ‖² e^{−Δ a(β)(nb^k − s)} ≤ C e^{−Δ a_* n}, using a(β)b^k ≥ a_*. So m_* = Δa_*. ∎

Hence X carries **no information beyond ME1 restricted to coarse-resolution observables** (the restriction is the cyclicity issue O1 of D08). LATTICE failure mode "(X) is just ME1 relabelled" is **confirmed** as a statement. It does **not** make the route empty, because the route is a *method* for proving X (step S6). The question is what that method contributes. That is answered next.

## Proposition 2 (conditional reduction of the β-uniformity to a β-free statement; Conditional)
Let k_*(β) be the first exit of Balaban's effective coupling from ]0,γ] (S4). Define a(β) := a_* b^{−(k_*(β)−1)}, a non-gap, coupling-defined scale (D03.3-admissible). Assume:
- **(H-Bal) = R1-H01.** For every β ≥ β₀, the infinite-volume effective measure ν_β at scale k_*−1, i.e. the block-spin pushforward with a θ-equivariant B (R1-D02 Prop. 5), belongs to a class 𝒜_γ of measures on unit-lattice gauge fields. 𝒜_γ is defined by β-*independent* conditions: Balaban-type small/large-field bounds with effective coupling in (γ/(1+cγ), γ] (R1-E02: c ≈ 0.064 for SU(2), b=2) and polymer remainders decaying at a fixed rate.
- **(H-Gap) = R1-H02.** There are m_γ > 0 and a coarse-local family 𝒮′ such that every ν ∈ 𝒜_γ satisfies 0 ≤ ⟨θF̄′;τ′_nF′⟩_ν ≤ C(F′,ν) e^{−m_γ n}.

Then X holds with m_* = m_γ, and hence by D08.3, (U) on 𝒦_β with Δ = m_γ/(b a_*).

*Proof.* (H-Bal) puts every ν_β in 𝒜_γ. (H-Gap) then gives X with a β-independent rate. Apply D08.3. ∎

## What R1 adds, honestly
1. **A genuine structural gain: β disappears.** The asymptotic statement "uniformly as β→∞" (ME1) is replaced by a *fixed-coupling* statement (H-Gap) about a β-independent class 𝒜_γ. In the model flow, the remaining distance from γ to the IR does not depend on β (R1-E02: 152 SU(2) steps from γ=0.1 to the 2-loop pole, for every β). This is real content beyond relabelling. It is what makes LATTICE's L-04 idea (a computer-assisted step) conceivable at all, since one fixed problem replaces a family.
2. **But (H-Gap) is not easier than a known open problem.** Plausibly 𝒜_γ contains the bare Wilson action at β_γ = 2N/γ (the k=0 element of Balaban's class; Heuristic, unverified). If so, (H-Gap) implies an exponential-clustering (lattice mass-gap) theorem for Wilson SU(N) at one fixed weak coupling β = 2N/γ, uniformly in volume. That is open: Chatterjee S-122 Problem 5.1, and every proved lattice gap is at β ≤ β_max (INV-001 §4).
3. **By the U(1) filter (R1-D01 Lemma A), (H-Gap) needs non-abelian, non-perturbative input.** The U(1) analogue of 𝒜_γ contains massless theories. The target rate m_γ ~ exp(−1/(2b₀γ)) (Heuristic) is invisible to any expansion in γ.

**Summary label.** X ≡ restricted ME1 (Proved here). R1 = ME1 ⇐ [H-Bal: L-uniform Balaban up to the γ-exit] + [H-Gap: a uniform, fixed-weak-coupling lattice gap over 𝒜_γ] (Conditional reduction, Proved here given its hypotheses). The obstruction is H-Gap. It is open, at least as hard as a fixed-β lattice mass gap, and must be non-abelian and non-perturbative.

Failure modes:
- the crossover window has no small parameter at all, so it would need a computer-assisted (interval) step (my L-04);
- large-field regions break the strong-coupling class;
- (X) is just ME1 relabelled, with no gain;
- a(β) is circular (D03.3).

**R2: β-uniform Poincaré/LSI for block-spin measures.**
First verification checks:
1. E04 is reproduced. Bare Bakry–Émery fails at β ≥ 2 (Elementary: K = 2 − β).
2. QUASAR's proposed first test on the *2D decimated* measures is **degenerate**. In 2D the coarse measure is a product of independent plaquettes, so any functional-inequality constant is trivially β-uniform. It tests bookkeeping only.
3. Before any numerics, specify the coarse dynamics: μ′ may have no usable smooth density for Langevin, so a heat-bath on coarse links may be needed. Also specify the implication "Poincaré/LSI ⇒ time-direction exponential decay" for non-local μ′ (defect 6).

Failure modes:
- the constants degrade with L, because coarse non-locality breaks the tensorization;
- the Poincaré inequality holds but gives no decay without locality;
- the inequality is true for U(1) too, so it cannot imply a gap.

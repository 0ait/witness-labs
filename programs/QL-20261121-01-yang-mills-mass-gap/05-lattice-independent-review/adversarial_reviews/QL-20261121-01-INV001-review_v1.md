## B. Independent reconstruction
**Interpretation.** INV-001 asks what a lattice→continuum gap transfer must assert. It answers with an abstract spectral lemma (D01), its lattice form (D02), obstruction lemmas and counterexamples (D03–D06), and a conditional block-spin reduction (D08). No 4D gap is claimed. I agree with that reading.

**Definitions and assumptions checked.** I checked:
- OS reconstruction from a reflection-positive, time-translation-invariant functional S. This needs (B) or some sub-exponential growth in t for the contraction argument.
- H ≥ 0 with HΩ = 0, and Q = 1 − |Ω⟩⟨Ω|.
- Lattice transfer matrix T: self-adjoint, strictly positive, compact (Lüscher 1977, doi:10.1007/BF01614090, Crossref OK, text not opened). It has a simple top eigenvalue λ₀ by Jentzsch.

**Independent method, and how it differs.** Every lemma was re-proved by hand, from the spectral theorem only. Two of my proof routes differ from QUASAR's:
- **D03.2.** I use injectivity of e^{−tH/2}: from ⟨QF̂, e^{−tH}QF̂⟩ = ‖e^{−tH/2}QF̂‖² = 0 at a single t > 0, it follows that QF̂ = 0. QUASAR's route uses continuity at t = 0.
- **D08.1.** My route is the same Laplace-measure argument as D01 step 2, written for λ^n on [0,1].

**Core derivations (LATTICE, by hand):**
- **D01, the Laplace-measure step.** Let ν = ⟨ψ, E_H(·)ψ⟩ with ψ = QF̂. Then ν([0,Δ−δ]) e^{−t(Δ−δ)} ≤ ∫e^{−tλ}dν ≤ C e^{−Δt}, so ν([0,Δ−δ]) ≤ C e^{−δt} → 0. Hence ν([0,Δ)) = 0. Combined with density of the F̂, this gives E_H([0,Δ)) = |Ω⟩⟨Ω|.
- **D01, converse.** If ker H = ℂΩ and σ(H) ∩ (0,Δ) = ∅, then ‖e^{−tH}Q‖ ≤ e^{−Δt}. **Both** hypotheses are needed. Without ker H = ℂΩ the converse fails, since a second vacuum gives a non-decaying C_{F,F}.
- **D08.1.** Let r = e^{−μ/b} and r′ > r. If ν((r′,1]) = c > 0, then ∫λ^{nb−s}dν ≥ c·r′^{nb−s}. Dividing by C e^{−μn} gives (c/C)·r′^{−s}·(r′/r)^{nb} → ∞, a contradiction. So E_A((r,1])ψ = 0. E commutes with A, so it vanishes on the closed span 𝒦₀ of {A^jψ}. 𝒦₀ reduces A, so ‖A|𝒦₀‖ ≤ r. **A ≥ 0 is essential:** without it, odd powers can cancel. Lüscher's positivity supplies it. For even exponents, RP alone would suffice.
- **D02(a).** T commutes with Q and leaves ℋ^loc ⊖ ℂΩ_L invariant (T is symmetric and leaves ℋ^loc and ℂΩ_L invariant). So ‖(T/λ₀)^n‖ on that space equals e^{−m^loc n}. Then apply Cauchy–Schwarz.

## E. Adversarial findings
- **Edge cases.**
  - D01 with t_F > 0 is fine.
  - D08.1 with s = 0, or with A having kernel, is fine.
  - D02 with periodic time requires N_t → ∞ first, which QUASAR states.
  - G₂ (trivial centre): ℋ^loc may equal ℋ^inv, so m^loc = m^full. This is not a defect; the torelon caveat simply changes form (string breaking).
- **Counterexamples.** None to any proved statement. All of D04–D06 meet their hypotheses as checked.
- **Alternative interpretation.** "(U′) suffices without cyclicity in the full ℋ?" **No.** Without (H1), D01 yields a gap only on the closed span of 𝒟Ω (D06.2 is exactly this counterexample).
- **Hidden assumptions:**
  - Cyclicity of the contractible-loop (or coarse, scale ≥ a_*) family in the JW Hilbert space. This is a Hypothesis.
  - Hypercubic → O(4) restoration, used to move spatial decay into time-direction decay. D01 needs time-direction decay, which the transfer matrix gives directly.
  - RP of block-spin maps.
- **Numerical weaknesses.** E04 l=0 at β=256: QUASAR prints 8 decimals, but only about 6 are significant.
- **Not reviewed:** D07 (beyond E01), the literature claims (texts not opened), novelty (no search done), E03.

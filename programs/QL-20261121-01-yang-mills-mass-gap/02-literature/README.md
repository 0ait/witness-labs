# 02 — Literature

- [`source_register.csv`](source_register.csv): every source used by the program (S-001…S-128, plus committee statements and repositories) with title, authors, date, venue, URL or DOI, sha256 of the archived copy, type, status, key claims, limitations and access date. The internal `local_path` column was replaced by `internal_archive_copy` (yes/no); see `/REDACTIONS.md`.
- [`INV-001_literature.md`](INV-001_literature.md): the INV-001 literature baseline (transfer-matrix positivity, strong-coupling results, Balaban's program, Magnen–Rivasseau–Sénéor, stochastic quantization, abelian obstruction), with verified quotations and an explanation of why each does not supply the missing estimates. All DOIs were checked against Crossref.

**Not redistributed:** the Jaffe–Witten PDF and text extractions, the Clay web page, eight arXiv PDFs (1803.01950, 2006.04987, 2201.03487, 2202.10375, 2204.12737, 2401.13299, 2505.16585, 2509.04688), KITP committee statements, the Yang–Mills group's assessment PDF, and fetched third-party web pages. Their URLs and sha256 are in the register.

**Access limits (honest record).** Project Euclid blocked automated access; this was not circumvented. Lüscher 1977, Osterwalder–Seiler 1978, Balaban and Magnen–Rivasseau–Sénéor are therefore cited from abstracts and verified excerpts only, and labelled as such.

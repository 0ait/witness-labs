# Mission

Witness Labs investigates difficult problems in mathematics and mathematical physics, with an emphasis on rigour, originality and complete transparency about method and failure.

**Current program.** QL-20261121-01, the Yang–Mills Existence and Mass Gap problem, with emphasis on a rigorous mass gap. Completion condition (mission charter): a complete rigorous proof or rigorous refutation, independently examined and prepared for external scrutiny. No such result exists, here or elsewhere.

**Principles** (from the [constitution](constitution.md), Part X, summarized):
- QUASAR originates; LATTICE independently validates and innovates. Nothing is "validated" on QUASAR's say-so.
- Never claim an experiment, search or formal check that did not happen.
- Keep numerical evidence, experimental evidence, conjecture and proof distinct.
- Preserve uncertainty, failed approaches, counterexamples and unresolved disagreements.
- Maximize truth, rigour and useful innovation, not volume.

**Public archive.** Everything substantial is documented locally first, then, after the Commander's approval, published here as an append-only, chronological record. Unsuccessful attempts are kept because they are part of how the work actually progressed.

Full texts: [mission charter](mission-charter.md) (immutable) and [constitution](constitution.md).

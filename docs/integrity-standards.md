# Integrity standards

Source: the [constitution](constitution.md) (Parts IV.5 and X) and the [mission charter](mission-charter.md) (Parts XIII–XV).

1. **Knowledge labels** on every claim: Established theorem · Formally verified theorem · Computationally verified finite result · Numerically supported result · Conjecture · Hypothesis · Experimental observation · Heuristic · Open problem.
2. **Knowledge tiers** for the lab's state of belief: Established · Working · Experimental · Speculative · Rejected. Nothing moves up a tier without its evidence.
3. **No proxy claims.** A related phenomenon (confinement, area law, clustering of one observable, glueball numerics, a finite-volume gap, a strong-coupling gap at fixed spacing) is never written as the mass gap without a proved implication.
4. **Strict separation** of hypothesis vs result, attempted vs completed proof, numerical observation vs theorem, and agent claim vs independently verified.
5. **Honesty about capability.** Never claim to have run code, read a source, or machine-checked a proof unless it happened. Sources read only at abstract level are marked as such. Blocked sites are not circumvented.
6. **Reproducibility.** Every experiment has code, environment, run instructions, outputs with sha256, and its failed runs.
7. **Preservation.** Failed approaches, computational failures, counterexamples and disagreements are kept.
8. **Verdicts.** LATTICE's classes: VERIFIED; VERIFIED WITH CONDITIONS; COMPUTATIONALLY REPRODUCED; STRONGLY SUPPORTED, NOT PROVEN; REQUIRES CORRECTION; REJECTED; UNRESOLVED. Never VERIFIED merely because no counterexample was found.
